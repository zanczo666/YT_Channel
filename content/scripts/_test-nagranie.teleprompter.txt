L001  There is one test I run before I trust any trading system.

L002  It takes about ten minutes, and most strategies do not survive it.

L003  Split your historical data into two parts before you start.

L004  Build and tune the strategy on the first part only.

L005  The second part stays closed until the strategy is finished.

L006  Then you run it once on the second part, and you do not touch it again.

L007  Every parameter you tune is a small decision based on the data.

L008  Enough of those decisions and the strategy learns the past by heart.

L009  A curve that looks perfect on known data tells you nothing.

L010  The second half is the only part that was never allowed to vote.

L011  Run this split on your own system this week and see what happens.

L012  Historical results, not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
